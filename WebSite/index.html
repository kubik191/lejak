Prototype work examples

Real problems. Practical solutions.

Three examples from our prototypes, built around real workflows that keep people in control.

Booking and inbox, without the back-and-forth

Booking & automation

If customers still book by email or paper, we can put a real booking system behind that. An assistant reads the message, checks the calendar, holds the time, and sends a payment link. Recurring bookings are imported once. Staff can still edit everything by hand. You keep the email address and the calendar. We handle the repetitive replies.

Unusual requests, like discounts or special deals, go to a person, not automatic booking.

Data collection that watches itself

Automation & monitoring

If someone on your team still fetches data by hand, or a job runs overnight and fails without anyone noticing, we can replace that with a scheduled collector. It runs in the background, stores a clean history, and shows its own health on a dashboard.

It emails you only when something actually breaks. People get involved when attention is needed.

Software that does the tedious copying

Workflow tool

If specialists spend hours copying points, fields, or files from one place to another, we can build a tool that does the mapping, shows a preview, and imports the selected records with a backup.

The specialist still reviews and confirms. The tool takes over the copying.
