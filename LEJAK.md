# LEJAK

LEJAK is a tech agency started by two friends. We build websites, chatbots, and booking and automation solutions. We also consult and train people on using tech and AI.

We do not replace people. We help them work faster by taking over repetitive tasks, so they can focus on what matters. Owners save money. Employees save time and nerves.

If you have a slow, error-prone process, we can automate the boring parts and leave you in control of the decisions that still need a human. Here is the kind of work we can do for you.

## Booking and inbox, without the back-and-forth

If customers still book by email or paper, we can put a real booking system behind that. A customer writes in. An assistant reads the message, checks the calendar, holds the time, and sends a payment link. Recurring bookings can be imported once. Staff can still edit everything by hand. Unusual requests — discounts, special deals — go to a person instead of being booked automatically.

You keep the email address and the calendar. We take over the repetitive replies.

## Data collection that watches itself

If someone on your team still fetches data by hand, or a job runs overnight and fails without anyone noticing, we can replace that with a scheduled collector. It runs in the background, stores a clean history, shows its own health on a dashboard, and emails you only when something actually breaks.

People get involved when attention is needed, not for every fetch.

## Software that does the tedious copying

If specialists spend hours copying points, fields, or files from one place to another — zoom, click, paste, repeat — we can build a tool that does the mapping, shows a preview, and imports the selected records with a backup.

The specialist still reviews and confirms. The tool takes over the copying.

## What you get

The setting changes — a hall, an office, a lab, a map — but the offer is the same. We look at the work you already do, automate the repetition, and leave the judgment with the people who already know the job. We can also sit with your team and train them on the tools, including AI, so the new process actually sticks.
